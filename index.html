Five compounding issues keep mid-size accounting and audit firms slow, costly and capped in growth.

Too much manual work

Junior staff spend most of their time on reconciliation, invoice matching and ledger clean-up.

Reports rebuilt every time

MIS, variance analysis and working papers are drafted from scratch each period.

Growth needs more hiring

Firms scale by adding staff, so margins stay capped.

Tools stop short

Tally, Zoho Books and QuickBooks record data. They do not automate analysis and review.

Middle market underserved

AI audit tools are built for large enterprises. Mid-size CA firms are left without a fit.

Review-ready output, not raw data.

Aquvera plugs into the data a firm already has: bank statements, invoices, ledgers, GST/TDS records and accounting software exports.

Reconciliation

Matches bank and ledger transactions. Only exceptions reach a person.

Expense categorization

Sorts expenses using rules specific to each firm.

Compliance cross-checks

Compares GST and TDS data with filed returns and surfaces gaps before deadlines.

First-draft reports

Prepares variance analysis and MIS in the format the firm already uses.

From raw data to finalized output in five steps

  1. Connect data

    The firm uploads or links statements, ledgers, invoices and GST/TDS records.

  2. AI processes

    Aquvera reconciles, categorizes, cross-checks and drafts.

  3. Exceptions flagged

    Anomalies appear with a confidence score and source trace.

  4. Human review

    The accountant or auditor approves, edits or overrides each result.

  5. Finalize and learn

    Approved output is final. Corrections improve future accuracy.

Accountability is built into every output.

In compliance work, accountability matters more than speed. Nothing is finalized without a human sign-off.
